# Shorts Cutlist — Marketing Un-Silo

12 vertical clips extracted from the long-form. Each is 45-90 seconds, designed to stand alone AND drive traffic to the long-form + the PDF.

**Aspect ratio**: 9:16 vertical for IG Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts. Crop your talking-head + reframe the Excalidraw scenes.

**Universal style notes**:
- Open with the hook in the first 2 seconds. No intro music ramp.
- Burn-in captions, large, top third or middle. Cyan keywords. Magenta sparingly for the punch words.
- End card last 3 seconds: "Full breakdown in the link" or "PDF in bio." Logo bottom-right.
- Hashtags below. Use the brand voice — no buzzwords.
