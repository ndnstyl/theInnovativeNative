## Theme 6: Options strategies

- **048 HPnCLtM7HOk (Roll):** Defines rolling options:
  - **Roll out:** Same strike, later expiration. Loss is "offset" into new contract, NOT avoided.
  - **Roll down + out:** Lower strike AND later date (when you want to match stock's current price)
  - **Roll in/up:** Closer strike + closer date, higher risk, cheaper premium
  - **Adding a leg:** Take a new contract at different strike/expiry same underlying

## Theme 9: Where WT is WRONG or thin (for the critical assessment)

1. **Doesn't use RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, or any oscillator.** Zero mentions across 50 videos. He uses candlestick patterns, support/resistance, and multi-timeframe analysis. This is a gap in his kit relative to Mike's RSI(2)/IBS systematic approach.

3. **"Market goes up 76% of the time"** is a statement about daily/weekly return distribution, not a predictive rule. He wields it as a thesis-supporting mantra when he's down and says nothing when he's up.
4. **"Deep swing" is not a recognized term** — it's his rebrand of buying LEAPs slightly shorter than 12 months. No edge.
5. **Picks are talking-his-book.** He holds NVDA, META, GOOGL, LLY personally in both options AND buy-and-hold positions. Every video recommending them is compromised. The 80% NVDA sell video is rare honesty.
6. **Option math is hand-waved.** The $4 premium / $75 strike example is correct mechanics but his "formula" for choosing strike and expiry is never shown — it's a paid-course tease.
7. **"Stop losses don't help after hours" is correct but misleading** — applies to retail market orders. A GTC stop on Alpaca will still trigger at next open. He conflates "stop loss ineffective overnight" with "stop loss is bad."
8. **Multi-timeframe cascade (monthly → weekly → daily → 4h → 1h) takes him 30+ min per stock.** Not scalable. This is a manual, subjective process — exactly what Mike's automated scanner replaces.
9. **Survivorship bias on picks:** He cherry-picks winners in highlight reels (NVDA $83→$170, PLTR $27→$125). Losses get buried in portfolio updates.
10. **His "circle of competence" test is sound but doubles as a circular argument.** "Invest in what you understand" → "I understand it because I invest in it."
11. **Zero quantitative backtesting.** Every framework is narrative-driven. No Sharpe, no max DD on any "system."
12. **The Patreon/course funnel is the actual business.** Trading is the content + credibility layer. Mike should take the mental models, ignore the picks, skip the paid tiers.
