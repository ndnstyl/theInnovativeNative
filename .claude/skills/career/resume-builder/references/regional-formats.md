# Regional Format Rules

Adapted from jezweb/claude-skills. Default to US conventions unless the role is explicitly UK, AU/NZ, or Continental Europe.

## US (default for Mike)
- **Length**: 1 page strict (for < 10 years experience) or 2 pages max (for 10+ years; Mike falls here)
- **Sections order**: Contact → Summary → Skills → Experience → Education → (Projects if early career)
- **Personal info**: Name, city/state, email, phone, LinkedIn URL, GitHub (for tech). NO photo, NO DOB, NO marital status.
- **Date format**: MMM YYYY (e.g., "Mar 2024 – Present")
- **Education**: Degree, school, year. GPA only if ≥ 3.5 and within 5 years of graduation.
- **References**: NOT on resume. Don't write "References available upon request" — that's a wasted line.

## UK
- **Length**: 2 pages standard
- **CV term**: "CV" not "resume." Don't use "resume" in cover letter or email when applying to UK roles.
- **Sections**: Personal Statement (instead of Summary) → Work Experience → Education → Skills → Interests (optional)
- **Personal info**: Same as US but "town/city" sufficient (no county)
- **Date format**: "Mar 2024 – Present" or "March 2024 – Present"
- **Education**: List in detail including A-levels (or international equivalent). GCSEs only if recent graduate.
- **Interests section**: Sometimes expected. Keep brief and authentic.

## Australia / New Zealand
- **Length**: 2-3 pages standard. Australian recruiters expect more detail.
- **Sections**: Career Objective → Key Skills → Employment History → Education → References
- **Personal info**: Same as US. Australian work eligibility ("Australian Citizen" / "Permanent Resident" / specific visa class) is expected at the top.
- **References**: Often included on the CV (2-3 references with name, title, company, phone, email). Get permission first.
- **Date format**: MM/YYYY or "Month YYYY"

## Continental Europe (DE, FR, NL, etc.)
- **Length**: 1-2 pages
- **CV term**: "CV" or "Lebenslauf" (DE) / "Curriculum Vitae" (FR)
- **Photo**: Often expected in DE, FR, IT, ES (professional headshot). Not in NL, UK.
- **Date of birth**: Sometimes expected (DE, FR). Not in UK or US (illegal to require in US).
- **Languages section**: Required, with proficiency levels (CEFR scale: A1-C2).
- **Format**: More structured, often tabular layout.

## Mike's defaults
- US format unless JD specifies otherwise
- 2 pages (he's 20+ years experienced)
- No photo
- GitHub link in Contact section (for tech roles)
- Languages section omitted (English only is implicit for US)
- "Available immediately" or specific availability date in Summary if relevant

## Format flags by region

| Element | US | UK | AU/NZ | DE/FR |
|---------|----|----|-------|-------|
| Photo | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| DOB | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | Sometimes |
| References on CV | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | Sometimes |
| Visa/eligibility statement | Sometimes | Sometimes | ✅ | ✅ |
| GPA | If ≥ 3.5 | Usually no | Usually no | Sometimes |
| Page limit | 1-2 | 2 | 2-3 | 1-2 |
| Languages section | ❌ | Optional | Optional | ✅ |
| Hobbies / Interests | ❌ | Optional | Optional | Sometimes |

When in doubt, look at 2-3 LinkedIn profiles of people who currently hold the target role at the target company. Mirror their structure.
