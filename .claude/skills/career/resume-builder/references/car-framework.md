# CAR Framework — Challenge, Action, Result

Adapted from jezweb/claude-skills. Every bullet on the resume should map to a CAR story in the master CV.

## Structure

```
Challenge — what was the problem / context (1 line)
Action    — what YOU specifically did (1 line, active verb)
Result    — quantified outcome with provenance tag (1 line)
```

In the master CV, keep all three lines. In the tailored resume, compress to ONE line that contains Action + Result (Challenge becomes implicit from the role context).

## Compression pattern

Master CV (full CAR):
> **Challenge**: Onboarding new clients took 3 weeks due to manual contract setup.
> **Action**: Built a Zapier + Airtable + Docusign pipeline that auto-generated contracts from CRM data.
> **Result**: Reduced onboarding from 3 weeks to 2 days; saved 12 hrs/week. `[provenance: self-reported]`

Tailored resume (one line):
> Built automated contract pipeline (Zapier + Airtable + Docusign), reducing client onboarding from 3 weeks to 2 days and saving 12 hours/week.

## Action verb categorization (by contribution type)

Use the verb that matches what you ACTUALLY did. Don't use "Led" when you "Contributed to." See `verb-discipline.md` for the strength ladder.

### Leadership / management
Led, Managed, Directed, Coordinated, Oversaw, Supervised, Mentored, Coached, Guided, Built (a team)

### Creation / build
Built, Designed, Developed, Architected, Engineered, Created, Launched, Shipped, Implemented, Established

### Improvement / optimization
Reduced, Increased, Improved, Optimized, Streamlined, Accelerated, Scaled, Decreased, Eliminated, Doubled

### Technical / analysis
Analyzed, Architected, Modeled, Diagnosed, Refactored, Migrated, Debugged, Profiled, Instrumented, Benchmarked

### Communication / influence
Negotiated, Pitched, Presented, Authored, Published, Influenced, Aligned, Convinced, Briefed, Advised

## Domain-specific CAR templates

### Tech / Engineering
> Action verb + system noun + quantified scale + technology.
>
> "Architected microservices migration (Node.js + Kubernetes), reducing deployment time from 2 hours to 15 minutes across 50+ daily releases."

### Executive / Strategic
> Action verb + business outcome + dollar/percentage impact + scope.
>
> "Restructured GTM strategy across 3 regions, lifting ARR 38% ($4.2M → $5.8M) within 9 months while reducing CAC by 22%."

### Career-change / Transferable
> Lead with the transferable skill, then bridge to the new domain.
>
> "Translated cross-functional requirements between legal and engineering teams (5 stakeholders), producing technical specs that shipped 4 features in Q3."

### Recent grad / Internship
> Project-led; scope smaller but specifics critical.
>
> "Built sentiment-analysis pipeline (Python, spaCy) processing 50K daily tweets; integrated into product dashboard used by 8 team members."

### Career gap / Returning to work
> Bridge with concrete activity during the gap.
>
> "Architected family-business inventory system during 18-month caregiving period; reduced stocking errors by 60% across 3 retail locations."

## Anti-patterns

### "We" instead of "I"
> ❌ "We launched the new dashboard..."
> ✅ "Led dashboard launch as PM, coordinating engineering + design across 6-week sprint..."

### Responsibilities instead of outcomes
> ❌ "Responsible for managing AWS infrastructure"
> ✅ "Reduced AWS spend 35% ($200K/year) by migrating non-prod workloads to spot instances"

### Vague impact
> ❌ "Improved team productivity significantly"
> ✅ "Cut PR review time from 48h to 6h by introducing async standups and PR templates"

### Buzzword without substance
> ❌ "Leveraged synergies across cross-functional stakeholders"
> ✅ "Mediated weekly meetings between sales (4 reps) and product (3 PMs) to align Q3 feature priorities"
