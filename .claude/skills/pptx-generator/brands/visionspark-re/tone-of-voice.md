# VisionSpark RE — Tone of Voice

## Voice Character

**Grounded operator who happens to know real estate marketing.**

VisionSpark RE speaks like someone who has actually run listing campaigns, not someone who read a blog about them. The voice is confident but not arrogant. It uses data to back claims. It names specific problems instead of gesturing at vague benefits.

## Core Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Direct** | Say what it is. No throat-clearing. | "Video listings get 403% more inquiries. This pipeline makes them." |
| **Data-backed** | Every claim has a number behind it. | "Physical staging: $800-$2,900. AI staging: under $10/listing." |
| **Operator-grade** | Speaks from experience, not theory. | "This is the same pipeline running production right now." |
| **Honest about limitations** | Does not hide the "AI Generated" requirement. | "Every output includes mandatory disclosure. That's not a bug — it's compliance." |

## Do / Don't

### DO
- Use specific numbers ("403% more inquiries", "$10/listing", "15-minute turnaround")
- Name competitor weaknesses directly ("Ken Burns zoom-and-pan is not AI video")
- Show before/after comparisons
- Acknowledge the compliance concern and address it head-on
- Use "pipeline", "system", "workflow" — operator language
- Reference the existing production pipeline as proof

### DON'T
- Use "revolutionary", "game-changing", "disruptive", "unlock your potential"
- Promise specific ROI or income ("make $10K/month with AI listings")
- Use excessive exclamation marks or emoji
- Call the product "AI magic" or "one-click solution"
- Minimize the learning curve — be honest that there is setup work
- Use "we" when talking about the user's business — it's their pipeline, not ours

## Vocabulary

### Preferred Terms
- "generative video" (not "AI video" — too generic)
- "pipeline" (not "tool" or "platform")
- "staging" (not "enhancement" or "transformation")
- "compliance" (not "legal stuff" or "fine print")
- "disclosure overlay" (not "watermark")
- "property walkthrough" (not "virtual tour" — different product)

### Banned Terms
- "game-changer"
- "revolutionary"
- "unlock"
- "effortless"
- "magic"
- "disrupt"
- "crush it"
- "10x"
